# 0.1.42 — 从学习目标重置讲义规划 / Goal-driven handout planning

讲义先读取来源，再从学习目标确定必要的知识、方法、关键推理与代表题，最后整章成稿。阅读批次只随来源容量变化，不再按原文字数增加正文片段并拼接。重复或无关材料可以说明理由后省略，仍保留来源并核对其内容。

Handouts read sources, select the knowledge, methods, decisive reasoning and representative problems required by the learning goal, then draft a whole chapter. Source volume controls reading batches rather than prose fragments to concatenate. Repeated or irrelevant material may be excluded with reasons while preserving and checking the sources.

写作者先收到所选的逐字证据，必要时请求展开完整原页；展开不增加教学任务。短讲义同样执行独立取舍规划。正常恢复、取消、检查点和异常审查保留，未增加恢复配额。旧笔记不自动重写，使用“调整并重新生成”才能重置旧规划。

Writers initially receive selected verbatim evidence and may request full pages without adding teaching tasks. Short handouts also receive independent selection. Recovery, cancellation, checkpoints and anomaly review remain, without new recovery quotas. Existing notes are preserved; use explicit adjustment and regeneration to replace an old plan.

同时纳入前一轮格式恢复修正：确定性的 JSON 标点修补保持字符串不变；歧义反斜杠由恢复控制器明确选择后局部修补；全局交付要求不再强制逐章挂覆盖标签。历史真实结果与离线回放分开记录。

This release also includes the preceding format-recovery corrections: deterministic JSON punctuation repair preserves string values, ambiguous backslashes require explicit controller selection, and global delivery requirements no longer require redundant per-chapter coverage labels. Historical live results remain distinct from offline replay.

离线测试验证同一知识材料重复 40 倍不增加写作任务，以及来源展开、资料取舍、无教学内容与新增方法的边界。冻结提示实验和完整系统评测分别运行，不把模拟输出当真实生成质量。此次安装包验收不调用付费模型，也不证明成稿密度或费用已经改善；短章节新增的规划调用同样有成本。

Offline tests verify unchanged writing-task counts under forty-fold equivalent source volume, plus evidence expansion, source selection, non-teaching material and additional methods. Frozen-prompt experiments and full-system evaluations remain separate. Installer verification makes no paid model calls and does not establish improved prose density or cost; short chapters also incur the added selection call.
